Research Philosophy • Adult 18+ Scope

Why We Are Exploring Project Suna

In modern life, people frequently carry unspoken emotional weight. Project Suna is an exploratory research inquiry into whether calm, pseudonymous, non-clinical spaces can help adults speak what feels heavy before it turns into chronic isolation.

Status Check: Project Suna is an early-stage research inquiry, not an active or live product. We do not provide therapy, psychiatric treatment, or crisis intervention.

The Missing Middle Ground

Today, our conversations tend to be divided into two stark extremes:

  • 1. Everyday social circles: Friends, partners, and coworkers whom we often fear burdening, exhausting, or worrying with our private struggles.
  • 2. Clinical therapy & psychiatric care: Essential, specialized healthcare services that can involve long waiting lists, high costs, and formal diagnoses.

Between these two poles lies a vast middle territory: the ordinary, overwhelming days when someone simply needs a quiet, thoughtful listener to untangle their thoughts. We are researching what non-clinical, structured peer conversation support could look like in that middle space.

Our Core Research Values

  • Calm over Engagement: We reject addictive notifications, gamified streaks, and artificial urgency. Reflection requires quietness.
  • Radical Honesty: We will never describe unbuilt features as available. We will never use the word "anonymous" to hide technical reality, nor promise absolute confidentiality.
  • Safety First: Clear boundaries are the foundation of psychological safety. Non-clinical spaces must never attempt to substitute for clinical care or emergency crisis response.
  • Human-Centered: Technology should gently point you back to the real world, to your own voice, and to real people in your life.
