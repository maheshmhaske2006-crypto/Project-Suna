    Trust isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

    Care, without pressure.

    We're building the concept before opening a live service. The real product must earn trust through clear rules, minimal data collection, and accountable safety processes.

    • Clear about privacyNo promise of complete anonymity. A live service must explain what it collects and how deletion works.
    • Listening isn't diagnosisSupportive conversations and reflection tools are not a substitute for professional care.
    • Adults 18+ to startAny live community needs consent, boundaries, reporting, moderation, and escalation processes before launch.
    • No pressure to discloseDon't share personal or identifying details just to try a prototype.
    A note from the team

    We're building thoughtfully, not pretending to have every answer.

    This website is an interactive front-end prototype. The reflection and phrase tools work in your browser; live listeners, accounts, private messaging, and professional referrals are not available yet.
