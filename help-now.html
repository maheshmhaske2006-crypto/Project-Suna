Emergency & Crisis Support

Immediate Support & Helplines

If you are in immediate danger, feeling unable to keep yourself safe, or experiencing an acute psychiatric emergency, please do not use this website. Reach out to trained professionals who can assist you right now.

If you are in immediate physical danger

Please contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately:

  • United States & Canada: Call 911
  • United Kingdom: Call 999 or 112 (or 111 for urgent mental health guidance)
  • European Union: Call 112
  • Australia: Call 000
  • India: Call 112
  • Other regions: Please contact your country’s emergency dispatch immediately.

Established Crisis Directories & Hotlines

The following international directories connect you directly to free, confidential crisis counselors in your country or language:

Find A Helpline

A comprehensive, independent international registry of free, confidential helplines across 130+ countries.

  • Website: findahelpline.com
  • Coverage: Worldwide, searchable by country and specific need.

Befrienders Worldwide

An international network of volunteer centres providing non-judgmental listening to people in distress.

  • Website: befrienders.org
  • Coverage: 32 countries and multiple languages.

International Association for Suicide Prevention (IASP)

Official global directory of crisis centers and prevention hotlines maintained by IASP.

  • Website: iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres
  • Coverage: Global database sorted by continent and nation.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (USA & Canada)

Free, confidential 24/7 call and text routing to trained local crisis counselors.

  • Phone / Text: Dial or text 988 (English & Spanish)
  • Website: 988lifeline.org
  • Coverage: United States and Canada.

Crisis Text Line

Free, 24/7 mental health support via SMS text with trained crisis volunteers.

  • United States & Canada: Text HOME to 741741
  • United Kingdom: Text SHOUT to 85258
  • Website: crisistextline.org
