This document outlines the plain-language privacy guidelines for the exploratory research phase of Project Suna. It must be formally reviewed and finalized by legal and ethics advisors before any public service or active research trial is conducted.

Data & Privacy

Privacy Principles & Data Notice

We believe that privacy should be explained in clear, plain language. We do not use dark patterns, tracking pixels, or surveillance advertising.

1. What We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

On this website: We collect zero personal data. We do not use analytics cookies, marketing trackers, session replay scripts, or local database caches. Browsing this website is completely non-logged.

In our research interest form: If you voluntarily decide to complete the external research survey, the only data we receive is:

  • • An optional contact address (email or handle), only if you choose to provide one.
  • • Your confirmation that you are 18 years of age or older (required).
  • • Your single multiple-choice answer regarding support formats.
  • • Your Yes/No answer regarding willingness to participate in a research interview.
  • • Your explicit research consent confirmation.

We deliberately do not provide open text boxes in the survey to avoid collecting unsolicited private medical records or sensitive personal confessions.

2. Why We Collect It

Any feedback submitted via the research form is used solely for exploratory design research:

  • • To understand whether pseudonymous, non-clinical listening spaces are genuinely needed.
  • • To invite willing adults (18+) to 30-minute qualitative interviews about peer communication.
  • • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your contact info with third parties or data brokers. Ever.

3. Data Retention

Research responses will be retained only for the duration of the exploratory research cycle (not to exceed 12 months). After the study concludes, all contact addresses and associated records will be permanently purged from our spreadsheets and form storage.

4. How to Withdraw or Delete Your Data

Your participation is 100% voluntary. You may withdraw your consent or request the immediate deletion of your submitted survey responses at any time.

To request deletion, contact the research coordinator at:
research-privacy@example.org (Placeholder address — to be updated by founder)

Simply state the email address you submitted, and we will purge your response within 5 business days without questioning your decision.

5. Third-Party Tools Used

Because we keep our technical footprint minimal, we limit third-party infrastructure to essential static hosting:

  • • Static Web Hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify): Serves static HTML, CSS, and JS files. Basic server access logs (IP addresses) are processed by the hosting provider strictly for security and DDoS defense per their standard platform terms.
  • • External Research Form Provider: The survey is hosted externally (e.g., Tally, Google Forms, or Typeform). When you open the form, that provider's privacy policy applies to their interface.

6. Pseudonymity vs. Anonymity: Our Clear Stance

We choose our words with rigorous honesty. We never use the word "anonymous" to describe Suna, and we never promise absolute confidentiality:

  • • No Absolute Anonymity: Digital networks inherently carry IP addresses, connection routing, and ISP logs. Promising "complete anonymity" online is misleading and dangerous.
  • • Pseudonymous Design: Suna explores the use of pseudonyms to reduce personal stigma and facilitate honest conversations without exposing your legal identity to peers.
  • • Safety Limits: In any human support environment, duty-of-care obligations and severe emergency escalation limits may apply when life safety is concerned.
